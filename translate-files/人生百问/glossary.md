## Style Notes
- Translate in a warm, accessible Dharma-talk register.
- Use direct address and spoken rhythm where natural.
- Keep Chinese proper nouns in pinyin with explanation on first use where helpful.
- Preserve the Q&A format with "问：" → "Q:" and "答：" → "A:".
- Preserve paragraph breaks and line structure.
- For enumerated practice components, use noun phrases (e.g., "refuge, aspiration, precepts, right view, śamatha-vipaśyanā").
- Avoid over-literal calques; prefer natural English.
- When a term has multiple renderings in the DB, prefer the one listed above for consistency.
