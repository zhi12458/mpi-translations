如何把企业家专项做得更好，让大家于法受益，进一步，把佛法智慧带到企业管理中？关于这个问题，我们前期有过一些思考，但不系统。藉由这次因缘，我对此作了梳理，和大家分享七点。  
How do we make the entrepreneur program better? How can we help everyone benefit from the teachings and bring Buddhist wisdom into their management? We’ve thought about these questions before, but not in a systematic way. Today, I’ve organized those thoughts and would like to share seven points with you.

1. # **营造禅意氛围，优化工作环境** {#营造禅意氛围，优化工作环境}

Create a Chan (Zen) Atmosphere; Optimize Your Environment  
我们做企业，首先要让员工喜欢自己的工作环境，在这儿感到快乐，受到尊重，才会敬业爱岗，把企业当作自己的家，积极主动地投入其中。但现实中的企业，对员工来说，多半只是一个赚钱岗位，为了养家糊口，不得不来。  
When running a business, we must first make sure employees like where they work. If they feel happy and respected, they will naturally take pride in their work, see the company as home, and give their best. But in most real-world businesses, a job is just a way to make money. People come because they have to, just to make ends meet.

原因当然很多，这儿重点谈谈空间环境，也是容易改造的部分。最理想的，是有绿树环绕、鸟语花香的自然生态，因为植物本身具有疗愈作用，可以让人放松。而在室内部分，比如在高楼中，可以营造一个富有禅意的空间，让员工在工作之余，来此舒缓压力，安顿身心。  
There are many reasons for this, but let’s focus on the workspace, which is the easiest part to change. Ideally, a workspace should be a natural setting surrounded by greenery, singing birds, and fragrant flowers; nature itself has a healing power that helps people relax. Indoors, such as in high-rise buildings, we can create Zen-inspired spaces where employees can unwind and settle their minds and bodies during breaks.  
就像我们所在的空间，从拈花堂、静心堂、云水轩到外部景观，大家来了都觉得耳目一新。这片建筑本来只是老仓库，我们通过设计，重新打造成富有禅意和东方美学的环境。很多人看到后，都刷新了对寺院的刻板印象，觉得是红尘中的一方净土，愿意来这里读书、学习、参加活动；愿意一来再来，把这儿当作自己的心灵家园。反之，传统寺院多半是千篇一律的殿堂，烧香磕头的信众，不少受过高等教育的人看到，觉得佛教是求求拜拜的迷信，只适合没文化的老人家，离自己很远，没兴趣进一步了解。  
Take this very space: Chan Hall, the Meditation Hall, the Tea House and the outdoor areas. People find the look and feel completely new. These were just old warehouses, but we redesigned them to be full of Zen and Eastern beauty. Seeing this, many people change their old ideas about temples. They feel this is a peaceful place away from the noisy world, so they want to come here to read，study and join our events. They keep coming back because it feels like a home for the soul. On the other hand, many traditional temples look the same—just halls where people burn incense and bow. When educated people see that, they often think Buddhism is just a superstition for old folks and has nothing to offer them. They don't feel interested in looking further.  
这就是环境带来的吸引力、认可度和粘性，我们要引起重视。对员工来说，白天大部分时间都在工作，如果对环境毫无感觉，甚至不喜欢，就会加剧对身心的消耗，难以安住。现代城市中，从车水马龙的街道到高楼林立的住宅，都容易让人焦躁、疲惫。如果企业营造一个生态环境或禅意空间，让人与自然、人与人、人与自己和谐相处，就能使员工的身心得到滋养，提升他们的满意度、幸福感，以及对企业的归属感，从而安住工作。  
This is the power of environment to attract, gain recognition, and create 'stickiness'—we must take this seriously. Employees spend most of their daylight hours at work; if they feel indifferent or even dislike their surroundings, it accelerates the exhaustion of their mind and body, making it hard for them to settle in. In modern cities, from bustling streets to high-rise apartments, everything easily triggers anxiety and fatigue. If a company creates an ecological environment or a Zen space that encourages harmony between people, nature, and oneself, it will help nourish employees’ well-being, boost their satisfaction and sense of belonging, and allow them to remain settled and focused on their work.

# **二、重视慈善关爱，建立良性关系**Focus on Compassion and Care; Build Good Relationships {#二、重视慈善关爱，建立良性关系focus-on-compassion-and-care;-build-good-relationships}

现在不少企业和员工之间，包括员工和员工之间，只是纯粹的利益关系，几乎没有感情联络。这种关系是单线程的，非常脆弱。员工会感觉自己被资源化、工具化，一旦工作稍不顺心，或是看到其他机会，就会轻易离职。如果这种现象频繁发生，势必影响企业发展。  
Nowadays, the relationship between many companies and their employees, as well as among employees themselves, is purely based on business interest, with almost no emotional bond. This relationship is 'single-threaded' and extremely fragile. Employees feel they are being treated as mere resources or tools; once work becomes slightly unpleasant or a better opportunity appears, they leave easily. If this happens frequently, it will inevitably hinder the development of the enterprise.  
如何才能增进彼此感情，让企业更有温度？作为管理者，我们要问问自己：我的心中只有利益吗？有没有把员工当作事业共同体，把经营企业当作应该承担的社会责任，有没有这份关爱和担当？  
How can we deepen our mutual care and make the company a warmer place? As leaders, we should ask ourselves: Is profit all I care about? Do I treat my employees as partners in a shared cause? Do I see business management as a social responsibility I must bear? Do I have that kind of compassion and accountability?  
所有关系都是相互的。从企业和员工来说，企业给员工提供了工作机会，员工也给企业创造了利润，有义务关爱他们；从企业和社会来说，企业能够生存、发展，是基于社会大众的需求，所以在创造利润后，有责任回馈社会。  
All relationships are reciprocal. Between the enterprise and its employees: the company provides job opportunities, and in turn, employees generate profits. Thus, the company has an obligation to care for them. Between the enterprise and society: a company’s survival and growth are built upon the needs of the public. Therefore, after generating profit, it has a responsibility to give back to society.  
有了这样的认识和发心，就能想员工所想，并设立相应的关爱机制，为有困难的员工提供帮助，让大家感到来自企业的温暖，增加员工之间的感情和凝聚力。同时，还要本着公益精神，利用企业的资源和力量，带领大家适当参与慈善，关爱员工，帮助社会。这样即能让员工感受到企业的温暖，同时也能培养员工的爱心、公益心和责任心。当他们具备这些素质，企业氛围就会改观，变得更有温度和活力。此外，还可以增加大众对企业的认同感，这远比广告更有力量。  
With this mindset and intention, managers can understand what their employees need, set up care programs, and support those facing difficulties. This will bring the warmth of the company to everyone, deepening relationships and building team spirit. At the same time, following the spirit of public welfare, companies should use their resources to lead employees in charitable efforts—helping colleagues in need and giving back to society. This helps employees feel the company’s warmth while also cultivating their compassion, sense of public service, and responsibility. When they have these qualities, the workplace becomes more caring and energetic. In addition, it builds public trust in the company—far more effective than any advertising.

# **三、重视文化教育，重塑人生价值** Prioritize Cultural Education; Reshape Life Values {#三、重视文化教育，重塑人生价值-prioritize-cultural-education;-reshape-life-values}

对企业管理者来说，通常会从实用的角度，谁能把事情做好，谁就被委以重任，偏向个人英雄主义。但要把企业做大、做长远，仅仅把事情做好是不够的，还需要良好的运营机制。机制决定了，你开的是小卖部，还是具有上升潜力的集团公司。  
Managers often look at things practically: they give big jobs to whoever does the work best, which is a kind of ' individual heroism.' But if you want a company to grow and last, simply doing things well is not enough; a sound operational mechanism is also required. It is the mechanism that determines whether you are running a small shop or a group company with rising potential.  
除了机制，更重要的是通过文化教育，把大家的思想统一起来。佛教中，是依六和敬建立僧团，通过制度的平等，思想、价值观的统一和利益的合理分配，起到共住、共修、共同解脱的作用，充分体现了和合的重要性。  
Besides mechanism, it is even more vital to bring everyone’s mindsets together through culture and education. In Buddhism, the monastic community is built on the principle of six harmonious practices. Through institutional equality, the unification of thoughts and values, and the reasonable distribution of benefits, it enables members to live, practice, and attain liberation together. This truly shows the importance of harmony.   
现在的大企业通常有自己的企业文化，即共同的目标、信念和价值观，也是基于这个作用。要把企业做大做强，这点特别重要。否则，员工多了之后，很容易各自为政，甚至产生内耗，难以把力量汇聚一处。从短期看，企业文化可以提升士气和工作效率；从长远看，则决定了企业最终所能达到的高度。  
Big companies today usually have their own culture, a set of shared goals, beliefs, and values—for this very purpose. This is vital if you want your business to be large and strong. Without it, when you have many employees, they will start pulling in different directions or even fighting among themselves, making it impossible to focus your energy. In the short run, corporate culture lifts morale and productivity; in the long run, it sets the ceiling for how far the company can go.  
很多企业家读过工商管理的课程，听过各种管理大师的讲座。我们是否想过，这些课程背后的文化是什么？换言之，这些术背后的道是什么？事实上，每种管理都有其文化背景和哲学基础。我们现在所学的，基本是来自西方的商业文化。前些年，中国企业界也提出儒商、禅商、佛商等概念，希望把东方传统文化带入现代管理。  
Many entrepreneurs have taken MBA courses and attended lectures by various management masters. But have we ever considered the culture behind these courses? In other words, what is the 'Dao' (the Way) behind these 'Shu' (the techniques)? In fact, every management model has its own cultural background and philosophical foundation. Most of what we learn today is based on Western business culture. In recent years, the Chinese business community has proposed concepts such as 'Confucian,' 'Zen,' and 'Buddhist' merchants, aiming to integrate Eastern traditional culture into modern management."  
我曾多次做过企业和文化方面的讲座，其中涉及的重要问题，就是如何看待成功？现代的主流观点，往往是以事业大小、财富多少来衡量一个人的成功，各类排行榜也让这一标准变得具象，似乎成功是可以量化的。如果立足于儒家文化，就会从人的德行，从修身、齐家、治国、平天下的角度，考量你是什么样的人，对他人有什么帮助，对社会有多少贡献，这才是人生在世的价值，成功与否的标准。如果立足于佛法智慧，则是从觉醒、解脱、建设人间净土的角度看待成功，只有断恶修善，圆满慈悲、智慧的生命品质，进而帮助更多人成就这样的品质，才是真正的成功。  
I have given many talks on business and culture, and a key question often comes up is: How should we define success? The modern mainstream view often measures success by the scale of one's business and the amount of one's wealth. Various rankings have made this standard tangible, as if success were something quantifiable. From the perspective of Confucian culture, however, success is measured by one’s virtue—through self-cultivation, family harmony, state governance, and bringing peace to the world. Success is about what kind of person you are, how you help others, and your contribution to society. These are the true values of life and the real standards of success. From the perspective of Buddhist wisdom, success is viewed through awakening, liberation, and the creation of a Pure Land on earth. Only by abandoning evil and cultivating good, perfecting the inner qualities of compassion and wisdom, and helping others achieve the same, can we find true success.  
所以说，接受不同的文化，决定了我们有什么样的成功观，也决定了有什么样的终极目标和做事方式。这不仅关系到企业发展，还关系到人生方向。从这个角度看，文化的重要性是怎么强调都不为过的。  
Therefore, the culture we embrace determines our perspective on success, as well as our ultimate goals and ways of doing things. This concerns not only the development of an enterprise but also the very direction of our lives. From this viewpoint, the importance of culture can never be overstated.  
在科技日益发达的今天，人却越来越找不到自己，越来越迷茫、烦恼、压力重重。解决人的问题，才是企业乃至社会健康发展的保障。作为修学者，我们已经从佛法中受益。可以看到，不论对人生的认识，还是对自身的改变，佛法都是究竟圆满的。因为它既有理论高度，又有实践方法，所以很多人通过短时间的修学，就在观念、心态上发生很大改变。  
In today’s world, with technology advancing rapidly, people are increasingly losing themselves, becoming more confused, troubled, and weighed down by stress. Addressing human issues is the ultimate guarantee for the healthy development of both enterprises and society. As practitioners, we have already benefited from the Dharma. It is clear that the Dharma is ultimate and complete, whether in its understanding of life or its capacity for self-transformation. Because it offers both theoretical depth and practical methods, many people experience significant shifts in their perceptions and mindset even after a short period of study.  
世上什么事最难？改变人最难，所谓江山易得，秉性难移。没有大智慧的话，我们连自己都看不清，改变自己都做不到，怎么帮助别人改变？对于企业管理来说，归根到底，是对人的管理。不解决人的问题，管理起来是非常辛苦的。如果我们能从读书会开始，引导大家学习传统文化、心性智慧，建立正确的人生观、世界观、价值观，相信因果，自律自省，愿意做一个有道德、有爱心的人，做一个乐于奉献、利他的人，企业管理自然变得轻松。  
"What is the hardest thing in the world? Changing a person is the hardest. As the saying goes, 'kingdoms may be won, but one’s nature is hard to change.' Without great wisdom, we cannot even see ourselves clearly, let alone change ourselves or help others transform? In the end, business management boils down to people management. Without addressing human issues, management becomes an exhausting struggle. If we can start with reading clubs—guiding everyone to study traditional culture and the wisdom of the mind, establishing a correct outlook on life, the world, and values, believing in cause and effect, practicing self-discipline and self-reflection, and to aspire to be to be moral, loving, dedicated, and altruistic—then business management will naturally become effortless.  
开始做可能要费点劲，也需要有些善巧，还要做到前面所说的创造禅意氛围、重视慈善关爱，培养员工对企业的感情和认同感。当他们切身体会到，这里和其他地方不同，确实让自己受益，才愿意了解这种管理背后的文化，愿意参与学习。这样的话，员工不仅能通过工作解决物质需求，还能得到精神利益。可以说，是企业对员工最好的关爱。  
Getting started may require some effort, creativity, and skillful means. It is also essential to implement what was mentioned earlier—creating a Zen-like atmosphere, emphasizing charity and care, and fostering employees' emotional connection and sense of belonging to the company. When employees personally experience that this workplace is different from others and that they genuinely benefit from it, they will become more willing to understand the culture behind this management approach and participate in learning. This way, work isn't just about making a living—it's about feeding the soul. This is the best way for a company to care for its people.   
企业家属于成功人士，这既来自个人努力，也离不开社会支持，包括政策、资源、劳动力、消费者信任等，有责任将这份成功回馈大众，所谓“达则兼济天下”。在创造物质财富的同时，也应该通过自身影响力，向社会传递正向的人生观和价值观。这是当今时代特别需要的。  
Entrepreneurs are considered successful individuals. But this success doesn't just come from personal hard work; it also comes from the social support—things like government policies, social resources, workers, and the trust of customers. Because of this, they have a duty to give back. Like the old saying goes: ' Success entails a duty to benefit all.' While creating material wealth, entrepreneurs should also use their influence to convey positive views on life and values to society. This is something the world really needs today.  
我们这些年倡导修学，之所以有那么多人参与，并积极推动，就因为他们从中受益后，看到这是大众的需要，社会的需要，就会主动来尽一份力。这正是观念改变的结果。不是我要做什么，让大家帮着做；而是让大家认识到，这是自己想做的，很有意义。企业管理也是同样，有了共同的信念和价值观，自然能齐心协力。这离不开文化传承。  
Over the years, we have advocated for spiritual practice and learning, and the reason so many people have participated and actively promoted it is that they have personally benefited from it. They see this as something needed by the public and society, which inspires them to contribute voluntarily. This is precisely the result of a shift in perspective. It's not about imposing tasks on others, but about helping them realize that this is something they genuinely want to do and find meaningful. The same applies to business management. With shared beliefs and values, people naturally work together with unity and purpose. This cannot be achieved without the inheritance of culture. 

# **四、选择正命事业，抵制违法行为** Choose Right Livelihood; Resist Illegal Actions {#四、选择正命事业，抵制违法行为-choose-right-livelihood;-resist-illegal-actions}

八正道是佛法修行的核心，其中的正命，指正当的谋生手段。作为企业管理者，尤其要对此引起重视。因为这不仅关系到我们自己，还关系到员工及各个合作方。如果从事的行业不正当，或是做的过程中不合法，从世间法来说，会触犯法律和道德；从佛法来说，则会造作恶业，带来压力、负担、焦虑，影响生活、修行和内心清净。  
The Noble Eightfold Path is the core of Buddhist practice. Among its eight factors, Right Livelihood refers to an honest and ethical means of making a living. As business managers, we should pay particular attention to this，as it concerns not only ourselves, but also our employees and all our partners. If we engage in an unwholesome industry or conduct business in illegal ways, then from the worldly perspective, we will violate legal and moral standards; from the perspective of the Dharma, we will create negative karma, resulting in pressure, burden, and anxiety, and affects our daily life, our practice, and the purity of our minds.  
什么是正命的事业？首先是遵纪守法，讲究诚信，这样的企业才能走得远，做得大。国外有很多百年企业，他们的成功都离不开诚信。中国本是礼仪之邦，最重视诚信。但在改革开放的浪潮中，很多人急于发展，一心想捞快钱，捞大钱，诚信就成了被边缘甚至淘汰的传统。其结果是，企业大量涌现，又快速倒闭，真正有口碑、有长远生命力的寥寥无几。很多企业注重品牌宣传，事实上，诚信才是品牌的核心，是最宝贵的无形资产。近年来，不少品牌陆续塌房，归根到底，就是塌掉了诚信，塌掉了大家的认可。  
What is business of Right Livelihood? First, it means abiding by the law and honoring honesty & integrity. Only such businesses can grow big and last long. There are many century-old companies abroad, and they owe their success to honesty & integrity. China, historically known as a land of propriety, has always valued honesty & integrity. However, in the wave of reform and opening up, many people rushed to develop business, eager to make quick and big money. As a result, integrity became a tradition that was marginalized or even discarded. Consequently, while countless companies emerged, they collapsed just as quickly. Truly reputable enterprises with long-term vitality are extremely rare. Many companies focus on branding. In fact, honesty & integrity is the very core of a brand—it’s the most precious intangible asset. In recent years, many brands have 'collapsed'—at the root, it was their honesty & integrity collapsed along with the public trust.  
其次是符合戒律。很多人觉得，遵守法律就不容易了，为什么要遵守戒律？不是给自己找麻烦吗？事实上，戒律并不是简单的约束，而是立足于因果，帮助我们远离恶行、规避风险的准则。遵守法律，让我们成为合法的公民；遵守戒律，才能净化心行，让生命生生增上。  
Secondly, businesses of Right Livelihood should align with ethical precepts. Many people might think, "Abiding by the law is already hard enough—why follow precepts too? Isn't that just making things more difficult for ourselves?" In reality, precepts are not merely restrictive rules; they are guidelines rooted in the understanding of cause and effect, helping us avoid harmful actions and mitigate risks. Following the law make us lawful citizens. Observing precepts purifies our mindsets and actions, helping us elevate our lives, lifetime after lifetime.   
佛法认为，生命来自身口意三业的累积，即身体、语言、思想的行为。简单地说，就是我们每天做什么，说什么，想什么。所有这些行为不是发生后就结束了，还会在内心形成积累。我们今天有什么样的兴趣、爱好，有什么样的心态、人格、生命品质，就来自身口意三业，来自我们的思想观念、生活方式、行为习惯。过去的行为决定我们现在是怎样的人，现在的行为又决定我们未来成为怎样的人。  
Buddhism teaches that our life comes from the accumulation of the three kinds of karma—the actions of our body, speech, and mind. In simple terms, it is what we do, what we say, and what we think every day. None of these actions ends right after they happen; they also leave imprints in our mind. Our present interests, hobbies, state of mind, character, and the quality of our life all come from the three kinds of karma—from our thoughts and views, our way of living, and our habits. What we did in the past determines who we are today. What we do now determines who we will become in the future.

每种行为都有两种结果。一是外在结果，比如做一件事，成功还是不成功，犯法还是不犯法，会不会带来法律上的隐患？一是内在结果，包括做事积累的经验，由此形成的心理，都会被忠实地记录下来。戒律让我们远离不善行，就能避免在生命中留下不良记录。这不仅会给当下带来麻烦，还会给未来招致苦果。比如从事和杀生相关的工作，为了眼前利益造不善业，就会感得多病、短命、堕落恶趣的果报，可能在今生，也可能在未来，做了就必定感果。  
Every action produces two kinds of results. One is the external result—for example, whether we succeed or fail, whether we break the law or not, whether it carries hidden legal risks. The other is the internal result, which includes the experience we gain and the mental patterns we form. These are faithfully recorded within us. The precepts keep us away from unwholesome actions, helping us avoid leaving bad records in our lives. Such records not only create trouble in the present, but also bring bitter results in the future. For example, if we engage in work that involves killing, and we create unwholesome karma for the sake of short-term profit, the result will be sickness, short life, or rebirth in a lower realm. This may happen in this life or in a future life—but once we do it, the result is unavoidable.  
如果说违法还有逃脱法律制裁的可能，但因果是客观规律，绝无幸免的可能。所以我们一定要选择正命的事业，既不违法乱纪，也不违背戒律，在成为合格公民的前提下，做一个清净如法的佛弟子。这样才能真正过好今生，并为未来生命形成良性积累。  
While it may be possible to evade legal punishment, causality is an objective law from which there is no escape. Therefore, we must choose a career of Right Livelihood—one that neither violates secular laws nor contradicts the precepts. On the premise of being a responsible citizen, we should strive to be pure, Dharma-abiding practitioners. Only in this way can we truly live this life well and create a positive accumulation for our future lives.

# **五、做事成为修行，商场变作道场** Make Work a Practice, Turn Business into a Dharma Field {#五、做事成为修行，商场变作道场-make-work-a-practice,-turn-business-into-a-dharma-field}

对参加过佛法修学的人，“让做事成为修行”不是一个陌生的提法。相关开示已有好几十万字，并结集为四本书。为什么强调这点？一般人做企业，只是为了创造财富，获得荣誉，得到社会尊重。但如果让做事成为修行，把商场变作道场，给我们带来的价值，就远不止现实利益了。那么，单纯做事和让做事成为修行，区别到底在哪里？  
For those who have studied and practiced the Dharma, the idea of "approaching your work as a form of practice" is not new. There are already hundreds of thousands of words of teachings on this topic, compiled into four books. Why do we emphasize this? Most people run businesses simply to create wealth, achieve honor, and gain social respect. However, if we let work become practice and turn the marketplace into a place of practice (Dojo), the value it brings us goes far beyond material benefits. What, then, is the fundamental difference between merely doing work and making work a form of practice?

## 1\. 创造精神财富 Creating Spiritual Wealth

让做事成为修行，不仅能创造物质财富，也能创造精神财富。在世风日下的今天，不少人为了获利不择手段，甚至把自己做到监狱中。更多的人，则是健康透支，心力交瘁。因为他们心中只有利益最大化，一切让步于此，最后虽然有了钱，但代价巨大。所谓“前半生用命换钱，后半生花钱买命”，更可悲的，是再多的钱也买不回命。即使能买回来，身心也有了不同程度的损伤。  
Making work a form of practice allows us to create not only material wealth but also spiritual wealth. In an era where ethical standards are declining, some individuals resort to underhanded tactics for profit, even landing themselves in jail. Many others find themselves physically exhausted and mentally drained. They care only about maximizing profit—everything else must give way. In the end, they may have money, but the cost is enormous. There is a saying: “In the first half of life, we trade our life for money; in the second half, we spend our money to buy back life.”. What is even more tragic is that no amount of money can buy back a life. Even if it could, the body and mind would have already been damaged to some extent.  
享用财富，既要有健康的⾝体，还要有良好的心态，否则只是挥霍而已，只能带来快感而非幸福。有句话叫“钱在银行，人在天堂”，其实更大的可能，是“人在地狱”，这才是最可怕的。到那个时候，银行的钱一分都用不到，但为赚钱造下的业，却要自己独自承受。  
To enjoy wealth, we need both a healthy body and a good state of mind. Otherwise, we are just squandering it, gaining only momentary pleasure rather than true happiness. There is a saying: “Money in the bank, but one is in heaven.” Actually, a more likely scenario is “one is in hell”—and that is truly terrifying. At that point, you cannot use even a single cent of the money in the bank, but the karma you created in pursuit of that money is something you will have to bear all by yourself.  
如何避免这样的悲剧？必须通过修学调整观念，在做事过程中，培养感恩、接纳、宽容、慈悲等良性心理。当我们的心态和生命品质越来越好，不论物质条件如何，都能过得幸福。反之，有钱时担心货币贬值，财富缩水；没钱时觉得人生失败，活不下去。有的人坐拥几十亿资产，虽然亏了几亿，还是比绝大多数人有钱，但他依然受不了。因为他的心里只有钱，容不得任何损失，结果只会让自己遭受无谓的痛苦。而那些拥有精神财富的人，就不会轻易被外在变化所影响。财源滚滚，能以平常心面对，不骄不躁；一无所有，也能以智慧接纳，甘之若饴。  
How can we avoid such a tragedy? We must adjust our perspectives through study and practice, and in the process of doing things, cultivate positive mental qualities such as gratitude, acceptance, tolerance, and compassion. When our mindset and the quality of our life improve, we can live happily regardless of our material circumstances. On the other hand, when people have money, they worry about inflation and loss of wealth; when they have no money, they feel like failures and cannot go on living. Some people own several billion in assets. Even if they lose a few hundred million, they are still far richer than most people, but they cannot bear it. Their minds are filled only with money—they cannot tolerate any loss, and in the end, they only cause themselves unnecessary suffering. Those who possess spiritual wealth, however, are not easily affected by external changes. When wealth pours in, they face it with an ordinary mind—not arrogant, not too excited. When they have nothing, they accept it with wisdom and find peace in it.

## 2\. 实现终极价值 *Realizing Ultimate Value*

我们做企业，是为了实现价值。但一般人只看到现实价值，缺少对终极价值的思考。所以很多有钱人特别怕死，因为他拥有的只是现实价值，一口气不来，一辈子的辛苦就与他毫无关系了。从这个角度看，确实让人焦虑、无奈、不甘。  
We run businesses in order to achieve value. But most people only see worldly value—they lack reflection on ultimate value. That is why many wealthy people are especially afraid of death. What they possess is only worldly value. When their breath stops, all their lifelong hard work will have nothing to do with them anymore. From this perspective, it is indeed distressing, helpless, and hard to accept.  
事实上，单纯的现实价值是渺小且短暂的。不论自我感觉多好，如果把视野放大，在地球七十多亿人口和无法计量的生命中，再有钱的人，同样是砂砾般的存在。在太阳系，地球只是八大行星之一，此外还有数以百计的星球，且太阳的体积是地球的130万倍。在银河系，又有1000亿到4000亿颗太阳般的恒星。而在浩瀚的宇宙，还存在上千亿甚至数万亿个银河系这样的星系。这些已是难以想象的天文数字，更惊人的是，宇宙中可观察的物质只有5%，此外还有27%的暗物质，68%的暗能量。  
The truth is, merely worldly value is insignificant and short-lived. No matter how good we think we are, if we broaden our perspective—among the more than seven billion people on Earth and countless other living beings—even the wealthiest person is no more than a grain of sand. In the solar system, Earth is just one of eight planets, along with hundreds of other celestial bodies, and the sun is 1.3 million times larger than Earth. In the Milky Way, there are between 100 and 400 billion stars like the sun. And in the vast universe, there are hundreds of billions or even trillions of galaxies like the Milky Way. These numbers are already beyond imagination. What’s even more astonishing is that observable matter accounts for only 5% of the universe. The remaining 27% is dark matter, and 68% is dark energy.  
从这些角度回望，生命乃至我们拥有的一切，是多么微不足道。如果不关心终极价值，确实找不到活着的意义。即使拥有整个地球，又能怎样？和拥有一粒沙子，能决定沙子的命运，有什么本质区别？随着人类对宇宙的探索，很多人看到了生命的渺小，因此感到虚无。但佛法告诉我们，在有限的生命中，还有无限的层面。心的本质，就是宇宙的本质；宇宙有多大，我们的心就有多大。学习佛法，可以帮助我们认识生命的无限性。  
Looking back from these perspectives, how insignificant life and everything we possess truly are. Without a concern for ultimate value, it is impossible to find the meaning of our existence. Even if we owned the entire Earth, what would it matter? It’s like owning a single grain of sand and being able to decide its fate—not much difference. As humans explore the universe, many people see the insignificance of life and therefore feel a sense of nihilism. But the Dharma tells us that within this finite life, there is also an infinite dimension. The nature of the mind is the nature of the universe. As vast as the universe is, so vast is our mind. Studying the Dharma can help us realize the infiniteness of life.  
西方早期的天主教强调，世俗活动需从属于宗教目的，只有和升天有关的事，才是有价值的。这在一定程度上影响甚至打压了科学技术的发展。到了新教时期，这一观念有所转变。比如马丁、路德提出天职观，认为人是上帝创造的，只要做好本职工作，就是对上帝的荣耀。这就直接将世俗职业视为神圣使命，把现实价值和终极价值结合起来，促进了资本主义的发展和繁荣。  
In early Catholicism, secular activities had to serve religious purposes. Only things that helped people get to heaven were seen as valuable. This held back the development of science and technology to some degree. During the Reformation, this changed. Martin Luther introduced the idea of “vocation”: since humans are created by God, doing your own job well is itself a way to honor God. This turned ordinary work into a sacred calling, linking worldly value with ultimate value, and helped fuel the rise and success of capitalism.  
佛教又是怎么看待价值的呢？很多人觉得佛教脱离尘世，偏于消极，其实这只是一方面。对出家人来说，在一定修行阶段，确实需要远离干扰，精进道业。但大乘佛教更强调菩提心、利他行，需要投身红尘，以利益一切众生为使命，在利他中圆满慈悲，成就自己。从这个角度说，做企业和实现终极价值并不矛盾。  
How does Buddhism view value? Many people think Buddhism is detached from the world and somewhat negative, but that is only one aspect. For monastics, at certain stages of practice, it is indeed necessary to step away from distractions and focus on practice. However, Mahayana Buddhism places greater emphasis on bodhicitta and altruistic action. It calls us to engage in the world with the mission of benefiting all beings, perfecting compassion and accomplishing our own spiritual development through benefiting others. From this perspective, running a business and realizing ultimate value are not contradictory.  
只要在正见指引下，带着正念和利他心做每件事，既能做好工作，也能实现终极价值。观音菩萨的千手千眼，就体现了这样的大乘精神。此外，文殊、普贤、地藏等诸大菩萨都是这么做的，是我们学习的典范。  
When we do everything with right view, mindfulness, and an altruistic attitude, we can both succeed in our work and realize ultimate value. Avalokiteśvara’s thousand arms and thousand eyes express this Mahayana spirit. Likewise, great bodhisattvas like Mañjuśrī, Samantabhadra, and Kṣitigarbha all act in this way. They are our role models.

## 3\. 转商场为道场  From Marketplace to Dharma Field

现代人接受了达尔文物竞天择、适者生存的思想，认为人是由动物演化而来，同样适用这套规律。所以，商场就是你死我活的战场，充满对立，甚至是恶性竞争。还有企业倡导狼性文化，强调拼搏，如果带着这样的定位做事，从某种角度说，是在张扬生命中的兽性。  
Modern people have accepted Darwin’s ideas of natural selection and survival of the fittest, believing that since humans evolved from animals, we are subject to the same laws. Consequently, the business world is viewed as a life-or-death battlefield, full of antagonism and even vicious competition. Some companies even promote a 'wolf culture' that emphasizes aggressive striving. If we approach our work with this mindset, from a certain perspective, we are merely amplifying the animalistic nature within our lives.  
佛法认为，人有魔性，也有佛性，关键在于你张扬什么，成就什么。在做企业的过程中，我们希望得到什么？是张扬狼性，让商场硝烟四起；还是张扬佛性，让彼此合作、共赢、共同成长？取决于用什么样的文化。  
Buddhism teaches that within every person there is both demon-nature and Buddha-nature. What matters is which we manifest and which we cultivate. In the process of running a business, what do we hope to achieve? Do we want to manifest wolf-nature, turning the marketplace into a battlefield of conflict? Or do we want to manifest Buddha-nature, fostering cooperation, mutual benefit, and shared growth? The answer depends on what kind of culture we embrace.  
被誉为经营之圣的稻盛和夫，就深受中国传统文化影响，修学佛法，推崇阳明心学，尤其是致良知。在商业决策中，不把得失放在首位，而以公平、诚实、努力等道德观为判断标准，认为经营企业不仅要追求利润，还要让员工得到物质和精神的双重利益，并为社会进步作出贡献。最后创建了两家世界500强企业，并拯救了一家濒临破产的企业，使其重返世界500强。这些成绩充分说明，商场完全可以转为道场，实现经营和人生的双赢。功成身退后，他选择了出家修行，提升心性，以更好的状态迎接生命的终点。  
Kazuo Inamori, known as the “sage of management,” was deeply influenced by traditional Chinese culture. He studied Buddhism and admired the philosophy of Wang Yangming, especially the concept of “extending innate knowledge.” In his business decisions, he did not put profit first. Instead, he used moral principles such as fairness, honesty, and diligence as his criteria. He believed that running a business was not only about pursuing profit, but also about providing employees with both material and spiritual benefits, as well as contributing to social progress. In the end, he founded two Fortune Global 500 companies and rescued a third that was nearly bankrupt, helping it return to the Fortune 500\. These achievements fully demonstrate that the marketplace can indeed be transformed into a place of practice, leading to success in both business and personal life. After retiring from his business career, he chose to become a monk, deepening his spiritual practice and preparing himself to meet the end of life in a better state of mind.  
在我们的企业家学员中，有些在修学后转变人生方向，全身心地投入义工行，值得赞叹。同时，我们也随喜大家能安住现有岗位，做出一个又一个佛化企业，让自己在经营中转化心行，让员工在工作中得到成长，也让社会大众由此受益。  
Among our entrepreneur students, some have changed the direction of their lives after studying and practicing the Dharma, devoting themselves wholeheartedly to volunteer work. This is truly admirable. At the same time, we rejoice in those who remain in their current positions and establish Dharma-inspired companies. In doing so, they transform their own minds, enable their employees to grow, and bring benefit to the wider society as well.

# **六、践行去中心化，人人都是主人** Practice Decentralization; Everyone is an Owner {#六、践行去中心化，人人都是主人-practice-decentralization;-everyone-is-an-owner}

最初听到“去中心化”的理念时，我觉得很震撼，因为它体现了佛法的核心思想——无我。所谓中心，和我执有很大关系。因为我执，才会以“我”为中心；因为建立中心，又会进一步强化我执。但佛法告诉我们，宇宙是没有中心的，我们内在的觉性也是没有中心的。修行的目的，就是瓦解我执，去除中心。对于企业管理者来说，接受这一思想，有助于我们在做事中修行。  
I was deeply struck when I first heard the idea of “decentralization,” because it reflects the essence of Buddhism—no-self. A center is closely tied to our self-clinging. Because of self-clinging, we make “me” the center; and by creating a center, we reinforce that clinging. But Buddhism says the universe has no center, and neither does our innate awareness. The goal of practice is to break down self-clinging and remove the center. For managers, accepting this idea can help them practice even while doing business.

很多做企业的都面临两个问题，一是员工难管，尤其是年轻或有能力的人，稍不顺心就辞职不干；二是管理者思想固化，跟不上时代。随着企业的发展，管理者的重要感、优越感、主宰欲也会随之膨胀。事业做得越大，我执就越坚固，越不容易听进不同声音。久而久之，就会故步自封，成为企业前行的障碍。去中心化恰恰可以对治这两个问题，其作用体现在以下几方面。  
Many business owners face two challenges. First, employees are hard to manage—especially younger or more talented ones, who may quit at slightest dissatisfaction. Second, managers’ thinking becomes rigid, unable to keep pace with change. As a company expands, managers’ sense of importance, superiority, and control grows too. The bigger the business, the stronger their ego-clinging, and the less they listen to different opinions. Over time, they become stuck in their own ways and turn into obstacles to the company’s progress. Decentralization can help solve both problems. Its benefits are seen in the following ways.

## 1\. 自我驱动 Self-motivation

去中心化就是去掉唯一的中心，不再以管理者为中心，而是以项目或团队为中心。现在的年轻人见识广，有想法，崇尚自由，如果你想管他，那可不太容易。如果能提供机会，让他们发挥长处，自己管自己，积极性就会被激发。所以要创造条件，让有能力的人受到激励，充分发挥所长。这样不仅可以实现自我驱动，让员工高度自治，还能减少管理带来的内耗。  
Decentralization removes a single center. The focus shifts from the manager to projects or teams. Young people today are knowledgeable, independent, and value freedom. Trying to boss them around won’t work. But if you provide opportunities for them to use their strengths and manage themselves, their drive will kick in. So we need to create conditions that inspire capable individuals and allow them to thrive. This fosters self-motivation and employee autonomy, while cutting down the internal friction that comes from heavy-handed management.

## 2\. 平等、合作、共赢 *Equality, Cooperation, and Mutual Benefit*

去中心化可以把企业变成共同创造财富的平台。企业想要做大，应该搭建平台，把员工变成合作伙伴，真正做到人人都是主人，都有经营意识，得到发展机会。不是你为我干活，也不是我为你干活，而是大家本着共同的目标，平等做事。这样才能避免管理上的对立。如果只有你在苦心经营，制定规则，大家都在为你打工，别人买不买帐就很难说了。如果钱给得够，还会有人做；一旦钱少了，事多了，很可能就不做了。但为自己做事就不同，即使遇到困难，他也会想尽办法地做下去。平台大了，就要建立扁平化的关系，减少沟通环节，提高工作效率。  
Decentralization can turn a company into a platform for shared wealth creation. If you want to grow your business, you should build such a platform, turn your employees into partners, and truly make everyone an owner—each with a sense of entrepreneurship and opportunities for development. It’s not about you working for me or me working for you. Rather, everyone works together as equals, united by a common goal. This is how you avoid confrontation in management. If you are the only one managing with great effort and making all the rules while everyone else simply works for you, it’s hard to say whether others will truly buy in. If you pay them enough, they may stay; but once pay decreases and workload increases, they may well leave. But when people work for themselves, it’s different. Even when difficulties arise, they will find ways to push through. As the platform grows, it’s essential to establish flat relationships, reduce communication barriers, and improve work efficiency.

## 3\. 建立模式 *Establishing Models*

去中心化有助于建立模式化经营。因为没有中心，大家必须严格依制度做事，并有完善的检测标准，才能彼此协力，保障各项工作的顺利运行。否则，很可能是一盘散沙。这就必须建立一套完整的制度、模式、标准。模式的最大特点是便于复制，这样既能把企业做大，也能做得轻松。因为最累的不是管事，而是管人，如果大家认可模式，依模式做事，是很容易达成共识、解决问题的。  
Decentralization helps to establish model-based operations.* * Without a single center, everyone must strictly follow procedures and clear standards to work together effectively. Otherwise, things can quickly fall apart. This requires a solid set of systems, models, and standards. The key advantage of a model is that it’s easy to replicate. That helps the business grow while keeping things manageable. The hardest part isn’t managing tasks—it’s managing people. If everyone trusts the model and works by it, reaching agreement and solving problems becomes much easier.  
4\. 弱化我执 *Weakening Self-Attachment*  
去中心化可以弱化我执，这是修行的重中之重。我经常说，修行就是两个中心的转变。平常人都以自我为中心，但这个自我充满无明、迷惑，如果听从自己，就会烦恼重重。皈依三宝，是把中心从自己转向三宝，以三宝为依止，重写生命程序。进一步，还要以众生为中心，发愿利益一切众生。因为佛教不是为佛菩萨存在，而是为众生存在的。正如《普贤行愿品》所说：“一切众生而为树根，诸佛菩萨而为花果，以大悲水饶益众生，则能成就诸佛菩萨智慧花果。是故菩提属于众生，若无众生，一切菩萨终不能成无上正觉。”所以在佛教中，众生比佛菩萨更重要。因为佛菩萨早已成就，不需要我们做任何事。正是因为众生需要帮助，我们才有机会通过利他，积累成佛资粮。  
Decentralization helps weaken self-attachment—the core of practice. I often say that practice is about shifting two centers. Most people take themselves as the center. But this self is full of ignorance and confusion. Listen to it, and you’ll only get more troubled. Taking refuge in the Three Jewels means shifting the center from yourself to the Three Jewels, using them as your guide to rewrite your life’s program. Then you shift further to take all beings as your center, vowing to benefit them all. Buddhism doesn’t exist for Buddhas and bodhisattvas—it exists for beings. As the Gaṇḍavyūha Sūtra says, “All beings are the roots; Buddhas and bodhisattvas are the flowers and fruits. By nurturing beings with the water of great compassion, we grow the flowers and fruits of Buddha-wisdom. Thus, awakening belongs to beings. Without beings, no bodhisattva could ever attain supreme enlightenment.” So in Buddhism, beings are more important than Buddhas and bodhisattvas. The Buddhas and bodhisattvas have already completed their work and don’t need anything from us. It’s precisely because beings need help that we have the chance to gather the merit and wisdom for enlightenment through benefiting others.

可见，去中心化不仅是先进的管理经验，也是有效的修行方式，可以让我们打开心量，接纳众生，体会自他平等的空性。  
Thus, decentralization is not only an advanced management approach but also an effective way of spiritual practice. It can help us open our hearts, embrace all beings, and realize the emptiness in which self and others are equal.

# **七、重视因缘因果，提高做事效率** Understand Cause and Effect; Improve Efficiency {#七、重视因缘因果，提高做事效率-understand-cause-and-effect;-improve-efficiency}

“因缘因果”四个字，大家都很熟悉，但多半当作一个概念，没想过和做事有什么关系。其实，因缘因果体现了佛法对世界的透彻认识，蕴含着宇宙人生的圆满智慧。与此有关的另一个概念，是随缘。  
The phrase “causes, conditions, and effects” is familiar to everyone, but most people take it as just a concept, without considering its relevance to how we do things. In fact, this principle embodies Buddhism’s profound understanding of the world and contains the complete wisdom of the universe and human life. Another concept related to this is “going with conditions.   
说到随缘，很多人理解为随便、随意，或是无所谓、不思进取。其实随缘是告诉我们：要跳出自我感觉，客观看待每件事，尊重它的发展因缘。人很容易活在过往经验中，偏听偏信。但个体经验很有限，未必具有普遍性，由此形成的认识往往是片面的。现在有了大数据，可以帮助我们全面了解事情的相关因缘，由此作出的判断就会更客观。所以，随缘并不是消极，而是综合评估后的智慧选择。很多先进管理都蕴含这一智慧，这里简单提⼏点，以后有机会可以展开讲述。  
When people talk about “going with conditions,” many take it to mean being casual, doing whatever, being indifferent, or lacking initiative. In fact, “going with conditions” tells us to step out of our own subjective feelings, look at things objectively, and respect the causal conditions of each situation. We easily live trapped in past experiences, listening only to certain voices. But individual experience is quite limited and not necessarily universal, so the views we form are often one-sided. Now with big data, we can gain a more comprehensive understanding of the relevant conditions, and the judgments we make become more objective. Therefore, going with conditions is not passive resignation; it is a wise choice made after a thorough assessment. Many advanced management approaches embody this wisdom. I will briefly mention a few points here, and we may explore them further another time.

## 1\. 随缘可以提高做事效率 Going with conditions makes work more efficient. 

很多事都是由各种因缘造就的，在做的过程中，也有很多方法可以完成，但总有一种是直接、有效、事半功倍的。如果看不清主次，找不到关键环节，即使把事做成，也会费时、费力、费钱。如果看错，还会南辕北辙。随缘，可以帮助我们找到成功最直接的因，通过最近的路线，最快、最省力地完成工作。  
Many things come into being due to various causes and conditions. In the process of doing things, there are many possible methods, but there is always one that is direct, effective, and gives the greatest result with the least effort. If we fail to see what is primary and secondary, or fail to find the key link, we may get the job done, but only at great cost in time, energy, and money. If we misjudge, we may even go in the opposite direction. Going with conditions helps us find the most direct cause for success and complete our work by the shortest route—fastest and with the least effort.

## 2\. 随缘是对因缘因果的管理 Going with conditions means managing causes, conditions, and results.

现在的项目管理，很符合佛教的缘起观。因为做事就是由因感果的过程，通过创造因缘，最终达成结果。很多人觉得：我也在创造因缘，却还是不理想，很难达到预期效果。为什么会这样？往往是对因缘的认识、管理、跟踪不足，无法优化因缘，让事情越做越好。项目管理，就是针对某项工作，通过大量的经验总结，从各个维度形成一套管理机制和知识库。对整个做事过程，从立项到计划、跟踪、监管、总结，建立有效的次第和方法，使我们充分了解做好一件事的因缘因果，这是很有帮助的。  
Modern project management aligns well with the Buddhist view of dependent origination. Doing any work is a process of bringing about results through causes and conditions—by creating the right conditions, we eventually achieve the outcome. Many people feel, “I am also creating conditions, but things still don’t turn out as expected. Why is that?” The reason is often insufficient understanding, management, and follow-through on the relevant conditions, so they cannot optimize the conditions and make things better over time. Project management, through extensive experience and summary, establishes a set of management systems and knowledge bases from various dimensions. It creates effective steps and methods for the entire process，from initiation to planning, tracking, supervision, and evaluation—helping us fully understand the causes, conditions, and effects involved in doing a task well. This is very helpful.  
一般人做事往往只重视结果，但从佛教徒的角度，是因上努力，果上随缘。做事前，先检查相关条件是否具备，对不足的部分加以补充，然后努力去做。在此过程中，每一次都尽心尽力，对每一个结果都以平常心接纳。如果不理想，就把结果作为因，继续深入、优化，从1.0升级到2.0、3.0。总之，项目管理就是对因缘因果的管理，是对随缘的具体实践。  
Most people care only about results. But from a Buddhist view, we work on the causes and let go of attachment to outcomes. Before acting, check if the conditions are there, fill in what’s missing, then do your best. Every time, give it your all, and accept whatever happens with a calm mind. If the result isn’t good, use it as a starting point, improve things, and upgrade from 1.0 to 2.0, then 3.0. So project management is really about managing causes, conditions, and effects. It’s putting “going with conditions” into practice.

## 3\. 随缘是把竞争变成共赢 Going with conditions turns competition into win-win

做每个行业都会有同行，如何面对这些关系？是竞争，还是双赢？取决于我们要独占蛋糕，还是做大蛋糕。这两种观念都来自对因缘的观察和了解。有些人认为利益是固定的，你分一点，我就会少一点，从而导致对立、排斥。事实上，世间一切都是众缘和合，而非固定不变的。如果能建立善缘，把竞争对手变成合作伙伴，优势互补，创造更大价值，最终都能有更多收获。当我们本着开放的心，长远的眼光，和大家友好合作，做事一定会越来越容易，所谓得道多助。反之，每天想着和人竞争，绞尽脑汁，你死我活，既辛苦，也心苦。  
In every industry, there are peers. How do we relate to them? Is it competition or mutual benefit? It depends on whether we want to keep the pie to ourselves or make it bigger. Both views come from observing and understanding causes and conditions. Some people think that benefits are fixed—if you take a piece, I get less. This leads to confrontation and rejection. In fact, nothing in the world is fixed; everything arises from the coming together of many conditions. If we can create good connections, turn competitors into partners, complement each other’s strengths, and create greater value, then in the end everyone can gain more. When we work with an open mind, a long-term perspective, and friendly cooperation, things become easier and easier. As the saying goes, “Those who follow the way receive much support.” On the other hand, if we constantly think about fighting competitors, racking our brains in a life-or-death struggle, we end up physically and mentally exhausted.

## 4\. 随缘就是以他人为中心 Going with conditions means putting others at the center

随缘是以用户、员工为中心，建立自下而上的管理机制，而不是活在自我为中心的设定中。今天的社会瞬息万变，如果不了解因缘，就不知道社会大众到底要什么。有些老一代的企业家之所以跟不上时代，逐步被淘汰，就是被过往经验束缚，自以为是。反之，有些新兴企业之所以能快速做起来，抓住用户，因为他们能根据因缘变化，以市场为导向，建立适应社会发展的机制，使产品不断优化。  
Going with conditions is about putting users and employees at the center and establishing a bottom-up management system, rather than living within a self-centered framework. Today’s society is changing rapidly. If we do not understand causes and conditions, we will not know what people truly need. Some older generation entrepreneurs fall behind the times and are gradually eliminated because they are bound by past experiences and stuck in their own assumptions. On the other hand, some emerging enterprises are able to grow quickly and win over users because they adapt to changing conditions, take the market as their guide, establish mechanisms that align with social development, and continuously improve their products.  
随缘的智慧非常丰富。可以说，所有先进管理都没有跳出因缘因果的范畴。从佛法修行来说，随缘可以弱化我执，完成无我利他的修行。  
The wisdom of “going with conditions” is very rich. You can say no form of advanced management falls outside the scope of causes, conditions, and effects. From the perspective of Buddhist practice, going with conditions can weaken self-attachment and fulfill the practice of selflessness and benefiting others.

# **八、结束语 Closing Words** {#八、结束语-closing-words}

以上，就如何做好佛化企业提供了几点思考。一是营造禅意氛围，优化工作环境，让员工安顿身心；⼆是重视慈善关爱，建立良性关系，让企业具有凝聚力和向心力；三是重视文化教育，使企业和员业建立共同的信念和价值；四是选择正命事业，抵制违法行为，不被眼前利益捆绑；五是让做事成为修行，商场变作道场，让事业成就和精神修行融为一体；六是践行去中心化，让员工成为主人，激发大家的积极性和担当；七是重视因缘因果，随时根据因缘作出调整，在瞬息万变的时代站稳脚跟。  
So these are a few reflections on how to run a business inspired by the Dharma. First, create a setting with a Zen feel and improve the workplace—help your employees settle their body and mind. Second, truly care for others, build healthy relationships, and make the company a place of genuine cohesion. Third, invest in cultural education so that the company and its people share the same beliefs and values. Fourth, choose a right livelihood, steer clear of anything illegal, and don’t let short-term gains tie you down. Fifth, turn your work into practice and the marketplace into a spiritual field—let your career and your inner cultivation become one. Sixth, practice decentralization, make your employees true partners and owners, and bring out their initiative and sense of responsibility. Seventh, take the law of causes, conditions, and effects to heart, adjust as conditions evolve, and keep a firm footing in this rapidly changing world.  
这些管理蕴含深厚的智慧，如果我们的认识、修行、思想境界跟不上，是做不到的。通过学习佛法，可以帮助我们更好地理解这些内容，把佛法智慧落实到企业管理。在经营企业的过程中，把自利和利他统一起来，在实现现实价值的同时，实现终极价值。不仅给自己带来福祉，也给众生带来福祉；不仅给现在带来福祉，也给未来生命带来福祉。相信佛化企业的开展，可以给当代企业界人士带来启发和出路，解决社会快速发展带来的焦虑、烦恼，以及对生命的困惑。  
These management principles contain profound wisdom. Without adequate understanding, spiritual practice, and depth of insight, we cannot put them into practice. Studying the Dharma can help us better grasp these ideas and bring Buddhist wisdom into business management. In the process of running a business, we can unify benefiting ourselves and benefiting others, realizing both worldly value and ultimate value. This brings well-being not only to ourselves but also to all beings，not only in the present but also in our future lives. I believe that the development of Dharma-inspired enterprises can offer inspiration and solutions to today’s business community, helping them to deal with the anxiety, stress, and life confusion that come with rapid social change.
