## Chapter Title Correspondence

6. **Chapter IV English title inconsistent with English introduction wording**
   - **PDF page numbers:** Chinese p. 44 (body) / p. 4 (TOC) ; English p. 165 (body) / p. 103 (TOC)
   - **Chinese original:** 四、重生还是重死
   - **English original:** IV Focus on Life or on Death
   - **What is wrong:** The English chapter title uses “Focus on Life or on Death,” but the English introduction (PDF p. 105) translates the same Chinese phrase as “Cherish life or face death.” This creates an internal inconsistency in the English.
   - **Suggested correction:** Change the English chapter title to “Cherish Life or Face Death” to match both the English introduction and the Chinese chapter title.
   - **Type:** bilingual
   - **Priority:** Major
   - **Notes:** The Chinese introduction (PDF p. 5) and chapter title both use “重生还是重死”; the English renders the same phrase two different ways.

7. **Chinese Chapter VII title vs. introduction mismatch affects English correspondence**
   - **PDF page numbers:** Chinese p. 81 (body) / p. 4 (TOC) ; English p. 221 (body) / p. 104 (TOC)
   - **Chinese original:** 七、无情还是多情 (chapter title); 无情还是大爱 (introduction, p. 5)
   - **English original:** VII To Love or Not to Love
   - **What is wrong:** The Chinese introduction uses “无情还是大爱” while the Chinese chapter title uses “无情还是多情.” The English title “To Love or Not to Love” matches the introduction wording, not the literal chapter title.
   - **Suggested correction:** Either change the Chinese chapter title to “无情还是大爱” to match the introduction and the English title, or change the English title to “Heartless or Affectionate” to match the current Chinese chapter title.
   - **Type:** bilingual
   - **Priority:** Major
   - **Notes:** Requires an editorial decision; the introduction and chapter title in Chinese do not agree.
