5. **代词错误 their → our**
   - **位置**：PDF 第 155 页（内页码 151），Section III, chapter 2, paragraph 3
   - **原文**：The more we acquire, the stronger **their** fear of losing it becomes.
   - **建议改为**：The more we acquire, the stronger **our** fear of losing it becomes.
   - **类型**：grammar
   - **优先级**：Major
   - **备注**：Pronoun shift; subject has been “we.”

6. **逗号粘连/流水句**

   - **原文**：Similarly, our demands for better housing have grown rapidly, even in developing economies such as China, real estate development remains unchecked.
   - **建议改为**：Similarly, our demands for better housing have grown rapidly. **Even in** developing economies such as China, real estate development remains unchecked.（或插入 “and”）
   - **类型**：grammar
   - **优先级**：Major
   - **备注**：Run-on / comma splice; second clause is not subordinate.
