## Style Conventions
- Use "we" for shared human condition, "you" for direct address
- Active voice preferred; avoid passive clusters
- Plain vocabulary: "help" not "facilitate", "make up" not "constitute"
- Sanskrit terms: keep with explanation on first use
- Djot format: `*emphasis*` (single asterisks), `{% comments %}`
- Em dashes: `---` (pandoc converts)
- Section markers 【】 translate to plain headings
- Book titles 《》 use italics or plain
